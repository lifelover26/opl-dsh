# Kilo Inkling 2 Small

The live Kilo Gateway catalog publishes `thinkingmachines/inkling-2-small:free` as a separate free model from the older Pi route `thinkingmachines/inkling-small:free` and the OpenCode bare `inkling-2-small-free` route. Keep the exact Kilo ID in the Kilo adapter and display it as Kilo Inkling 2 Small; do not merge these routes or infer availability from another harness.
